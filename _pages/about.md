**Research:** My research primarily focuses on multimodal large language models (MLLMs), with an emphasis on enhancing their perception and reasoning capabilities through reinforcement learning–based post-training strategies. Previously, I investigated methods to accelerate MLLM inference by pruning redundant tokens based on internal information flow, as well as techniques to reduce object hallucination and improve visual grounding through test-time interventions. Broadly, my work aims to develop generalizable methodologies for building more intelligent, efficient, and reliable multimodal AI systems.

**Background:** I am currently a visiting student at [CCVL](https://ccvl.jhu.edu/), Johns Hopkins University, working with [Alan Yuille](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~ayuille/) and [Jieneng Chen](http://www.jienengchen.com/). Before that, I was a research intern at [Xiaomi](https://www.mi.com/global/) and [Tencent](https://www.tencent.com/en-us/). I received my M.Sc. in Data Science from the [University of Science and Technology of China](https://www.ustc.edu.cn) in 2025, advised by [Zilei Wang](http://staff.ustc.edu.cn/~zlwang/) in the [Vision and Multimedia Research Group](https://vim.ustc.edu.cn/main.htm), and my B.Sc. in Applied Mathematics from [China University of Mining and Technology](https://www.cumt.edu.cn/), where I worked with [Hu Shao](https://math.cumt.edu.cn/6d/ce/c9104a355790/pagem.htm).
