Our paper <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02994"><em>Video-OPD: Efficient Post-Training of Multimodal Large Language Models for Temporal Video Grounding via On-Policy Distillation</em></a> (co-first author) has been accepted to <a href="https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026">ICML 2026</a>! It brings on-policy distillation to video MLLMs, replacing sparse GRPO rewards with dense token-level teacher supervision.
